# Supplementary-Material-Union-Conference-2025-Poster
Supplementary material for the poster presented at the Union Conference 2025 on CSF penetration of pyrazinamide, levofloxacin, and linezolid in people with pulmonary MDR-TB, including exposure metrics (AUC₀–₂₄ and Cmax), parameter estimates table, and goodness-of-fit plots from the plasma and CSF pharmacokinetic analysis.
